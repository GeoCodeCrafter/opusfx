Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

OpusFX Poster is used only by the owner of the OpusFX accounts. It stores the access tokens for those accounts on the owner's own computer, and reads the account's follower count, like count and the view, like and comment counts of its own videos, to show them on a private dashboard.

It does not collect data about other people, does not sell or share any data, and uses no advertising or tracking. Revoking access in TikTok or Instagram settings stops all access; tokens are deleted on request.

Contact: through the OpusFX YouTube channel.
